## 2.3 \[NII\] and \[SII\] kinematics

- The forbidden lines are much weaker than H alpha and often their S/N is too low for a reliable characterization.
- In general the peak intensities of the multiple components of H alpha differ from those of the forbidden lines.
- Each forbidden line should be analyzed independent from H alpha emission.
- They are mainly used to verify double or multiple profiles in H alpha.
- Doubles tend to concentrate inside the large expanding shells; singles tend to delineate the high surface brightness borders of these shells; while multiple share the distribution of doubles.
