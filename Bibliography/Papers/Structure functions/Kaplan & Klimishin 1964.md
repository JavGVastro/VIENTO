# [Methods For analyzing interstellar Turbulence](https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/1964SvA.....8..210K/abstract)



1. 
$$
A_{n}(\alpha)= \overline{ \vert a(\alpha ')-a(\alpha '') \vert^{2}}=B_{n}(2R \sin (\alpha/2))
$$

2. 
$$
A^{I}_{n}(\alpha)= \overline{ \vert a(\alpha ')-a(\alpha '') \vert^{2}}
$$

$$
= \frac{1}{R^{2}}\int^{R}_{0}\int^{R}_{0} B_{n} (\sqrt{y^{2}_{1}+y^{2}_{2}+2y_{1}y_{2}\cos \alpha})dy_{1}dy_{2}
$$

3. 
$$
A^{II}_{n}(\alpha)= \overline{ \vert \frac{1}{R} \int^{R}_{0} a(y_{1},\alpha ')dy_{1}-\int^{R}_{0} a(y_{2},\alpha ')dy_{2} \vert^{2}}
$$

$$
A^{II}_{n}(\alpha)= A^{I}_{n}(\alpha) - A^{I}_{n}(0)
$$

4. 
$$
A^{III}_{n}(x)= \frac{1}{R^{2}}\int^{R}_{0}\int^{R}_{0} B_{n} (\sqrt{x^{2}-(y_{1}-y_{2})^{2}})dy_{1}dy_{2}
$$

$$
A^{IV}_{n}(x)= A^{I}_{n}(\alpha) - A^{I}_{n}(0)
$$
