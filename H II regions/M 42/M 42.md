- The Orion nebula (M 42)  is the closes Galactic H II region and corresponds to one of the most observed objects in the sky.
-  A reviews of the main features of the region is given in ODell  2001.
- The central part of M 42 is home to four massive stars, one O- and three B-type stars, that make up the Trapezium clusters of which theta 1 Ori C (spectral-type O7) is the most luminous. There stars are ionizing the surrounding material.
- Ionization fronts such as the Bright Bar and the Orion S cloud.
- Furthermore, because of the ongoing star formation, it hosts many optical Herbig-Haro (HH) and molecular outflows, as well as a large population of young stellar objects called proplyds.
- The three-dimensional structure of this region is that of a blister-like H II region in front of the Orion Molecular Cloud (OMC-1), where the emitting region is 0.1 pc thick.
---
- Elemental abundances in the Orion nebula using slit spectroscopy finding that the abundances of heavy elements in Orion are only somewhat higher than solar ones.
- Using IFS a common analysis is giben by line ratios of nebular emission lines which are used as abundance tracers as: \[NII]/Ha, \[SII]/Ha tracing the nitrogen and Sulphur abundance or as a traces of the degree of ionization: \[OII]/\[OIII]
- Another sulphur abundance parameter computed for both Galactic and extragalactic H II regions is given by S23= \[SII] + \[SIII]/Hb, which is commonly used to determine star formation histories and evolutionary scenarios, as this parameter is found to vary as a function of position within the galaxy.
- S23 and O ratos can be used to analyze the ionization structure. Together with velocity map, the combination has been used to detect a previously unknow outflow in Eagle nebula and has been applied to Orion.

##  MUSE

- The MUSE integral field observations of the Orion nebula were taken during the instrument's commissioning run on 2014 February 16. 
- The $6 \ \text{arcmin} \times 5 \ \text{arcmin}$ mosaic consists of $60 \times 5 \ \text{s}$ exposures, where each of the $30$ mosaic pointing's was observed twice with a $90 \degree$ rotation dither pattern.
- The data reduction was carried out in the `ESOREX` environment with the MUSE pipeline. Weilbacher et al 2012, 2015
- The observations were carried out in the wide field mode with a field of views of $1 \ \text{arcmin} \times 1 \ \text{arcmin}$, in the wavelength range of $4595-9366  \mathring{A}$ and a sampling of $0.2 \ \text{arcmin} \times 0.2 \ \text{arcmin} \times 0.85 \mathring{A}$.
