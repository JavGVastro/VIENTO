
# Equations


- Mass conservation:
$$ \frac{\partial \rho}{ \partial t} + (\nabla \cdot \rho \vec{v} ) = 0 $$

- Momentum conservation:

$$ \rho \frac{\partial \vec{v}}{ \partial t} + \vec{v} \cdot \nabla  \vec{v}  = \rho \frac{D\vec{{v}}}{Dt}=-\nabla P + \rho \vec{g} + \nabla \cdot \vec{\tau}_{ij}$$

- Navier-Stokes (Momentum conservation applied to Newtonian fluids)

$$ \rho \frac{\partial \vec{v}}{ \partial t} + \vec{v} \cdot \nabla  \vec{v}  = \rho \frac{D\vec{{v}}}{Dt}=-\nabla P + \rho \vec{g} + \mu \nabla ^2 \vec{v}$$

- Energy conservation:

$$\rho \frac{\partial \hat{u}}{dt} + P(\nabla \cdot V) = \nabla \cdot(k \nabla T) + \phi$$


# Euler

- Momentum conservation with $\mu = 0$

$$\rho \frac{d\vec{v}}{dt} = \rho g - \nabla P$$

$$\frac{P}{\rho} + \frac{1}{2} v^2 + gz = \text{cte}$$
