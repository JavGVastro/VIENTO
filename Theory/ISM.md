# Ionization structure of H II regions

- Halpha - All volume of the nebula since H is more abundant element.
- \[O III\] - central volume, stellar winds
- \[O II\] , \[S II\] - Borders, thin layers. Photoevaporative flow
- O stars -> \[O III\]
- B stars -> do not ionize \[O III\]
- B1 -> H II
