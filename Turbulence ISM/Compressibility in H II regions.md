
Here we study the relation between the density fluctuations and the velocity fluctuations on the plane-of-sky.


The RMS fractional width of density fluctuations in a turbulent medium [1](<On the density and emissivity fluctuations.md>), 
 
$$\sigma_{\rho / \rho_0} \equiv \langle \delta \rho^2  \rangle / \rho_0 \approx \sigma_S \ ,$$

is predicted to depend linearly on the Mach number as:

$$\sigma_{\rho / \rho_0} = b \mathcal{M} \ , $$

where $b$ depends on the nature of the turbulent driving [Federrath:2010z,](https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2010A%26A...512A..81F/abstract) varying between:
- $b = 1/3$ for solenoidal driving and
- $b = 1$ for compressive driving.

The mach number is determined as:

$$\mathcal{M} = \frac{\sqrt{3} \sigma_\text{POS}} {c_s} .$$
