
- Fluctuations in the physical properties of the H II regions are consider as follow:
	- Velocity fluctuations are consider from the fluid dynamic [turbulent](<Turbulence.md>) perspective (using as first approximation Kolmogorov theory).
	- [Density and emissivity fluctuations](<On the density and emissivity fluctuations.md>) are consider as equivalent.
	- Temperature fluctuations are studied using high order moments.
