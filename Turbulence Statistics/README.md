- In the analysis of astrophysical turbulence—particularly in the interstellar medium—statistical tools provide crucial insights into the scale-dependent structure and dynamics of turbulent flows. While direct measurements of 3D turbulent fields are not possible, we can infer their properties by analyzing observational data through spatial and spectral statistics. This section introduces and compares key statistical diagnostics, such as the second-order structure function, delta variance, correlation functions, and spectral-based methods like VCA and VCS. Each of these tools offers a different perspective on the underlying turbulent cascade, enabling researchers to disentangle the contributions of velocity, density, and instrumental effects across scales.
- The VIENTO project use its own [second-order structure function algorithm](<Structure function/Structure Function Numerical.md>) developed in Python as a primary tool for computations.

| **Tool**                                                           | **Focus**                                 | **Domain**                    | **Main Use**                                             | **Robustness to Edges/Noise** | **Related To**                      |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------- | ----------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| [Second-Order Structure Function ](<Structure function/README.md>) | Variance of fluctuations at lag `r`       | Real space                    | Turbulent scaling, field variability                     | Moderate sensitivity          | Power spectrum via `ζ`              |
| Correlation                                                        | Similarity between values at lag `r`      | Real space                    | Correlation length, smoothness                           | Sensitive to edges            | Fourier inverse of power spectrum   |
| Delta-Variance                                                     | Variance of structures at scale `l`       | Real space (filtered)         | Multiscale structure, irregular datasets                 | High robustness               | Power spectrum slope `γ`            |
| SCF                                                                | Spectral similarity between locations     | PPV cube                      | Detect coherent kinematic features                       | Moderate                      | Local spectral morphology           |
| VCA                                                                | Intensity power spectrum vs channel width | 2D spatial in velocity slices | Separate velocity vs. density contributions              | Moderate                      | PPV-space power spectra             |
| VCS                                                                | Spectral power along velocity axis        | 1D velocity                   | Turbulence characterization with poor spatial resolution | High                          | Line-of-sight velocity fluctuations |
| Power Spectrum (`P(k)`)                                            | Variance distribution over scales `k`     | Fourier space                 | Scaling laws, energy injection, dissipation              | Requires careful windowing    | Related to all above via transforms |
